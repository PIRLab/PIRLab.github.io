Build physical intelligence with us.

PIRLab welcomes motivated students and collaborators who want to work on robotics, computer vision, world models, and physically grounded action.

Join PIRLab

Research opportunities

We welcome enquiries from prospective PhD and MPhil students, research assistants, visiting students and collaborators interested in robotics and physical intelligence.

Applicants may have backgrounds in robotics, computer vision, machine learning, control or related fields. Relevant coding, mathematical or hands-on robotics experience is helpful.

Opportunities and institutional admission requirements vary by project and location.
